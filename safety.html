Safety Protocols & Procedures

Detailed safety protocols for different types of tours and activities

1

Wildlife Interaction

  • Maintain safe distance from all wildlife (minimum 25 meters)
  • Stay inside vehicles during game drives
  • No feeding or provoking animals
  • Follow guide instructions at all times
  • Use designated viewing areas only
2

Vehicle Safety

  • 4x4 vehicles with roll bars and safety features
  • Regular mechanical checks and maintenance
  • Emergency communication systems
  • First aid kits in all vehicles
  • Experienced drivers with park knowledge
3

Camp & Lodge Safety

  • 24/7 security at all accommodations
  • Electric fencing around properties
  • Escorted movement after dark
  • Emergency evacuation plans
  • Fire safety equipment and procedures
1

Student Supervision

  • 1:10 guide-to-student ratio minimum
  • Roll call procedures every 2 hours
  • Buddy system implementation
  • Designated meeting points
  • Constant headcount monitoring
2

Educational Safety

  • Curriculum-aligned safety briefings
  • Age-appropriate risk assessments
  • Special needs accommodations
  • Parental consent and medical forms
  • Teacher involvement in safety planning
3

Transport Safety

  • School bus safety standards
  • Seat belts for all passengers
  • Experienced school trip drivers
  • Regular rest stops and hydration
  • Emergency contact procedures
1

Water Safety

  • Lifeguard presence at all activities
  • Swimming ability assessment
  • Life jacket requirements
  • Tide and current monitoring
  • Designated swimming areas
2

Marine Activities

  • Certified equipment and operators
  • Weather condition monitoring
  • Briefings before all activities
  • Emergency boat procedures
  • Marine life interaction guidelines
3

Sun & Heat Protection

  • Regular hydration breaks
  • Shaded rest areas provided
  • Sunscreen application reminders
  • Heat exhaustion prevention
  • Appropriate clothing guidance

Altitude Safety

  • Gradual altitude acclimatization
  • Altitude sickness monitoring

Mountain Equipment

Emergency Procedures

Clear protocols for handling emergencies during tours

Medical Emergencies

  • First Response: Immediate first aid by certified guides
  • Communication: Satellite phone contact with base
  • Evacuation: Arrangement of medical evacuation if needed
  • Hospital: Transport to nearest medical facility
  • Family Contact: Notification of emergency contacts

Vehicle Incidents

  • Safety First: Move to safe location if possible
  • Assessment: Check for injuries and damage
  • Backup: Dispatch backup vehicle immediately
  • Repair: On-site repair or tow arrangements
  • Reporting: Incident documentation and reporting

Natural Events

  • Weather Monitoring: Continuous weather updates
  • Safe Shelter: Move to designated safe locations
  • Communication: Regular updates to base camp
  • Evacuation Plan: Activation of evacuation procedures
  • Post-Event: Damage assessment and recovery
