School Trip Planning Process

Our streamlined process ensures safe, educational, and memorable experiences for students

1

Initial Consultation

Discuss your educational objectives, curriculum requirements, budget, and preferred dates with our academic tourism specialists.

2

Customized Proposal

Receive a detailed proposal including itinerary, learning objectives, safety protocols, and all-inclusive pricing.

3

Approval & Documentation

We handle all necessary documentation, parental consent forms, school board approvals, and risk assessments.

4

Pre-Trip Preparation

Educational materials, pre-trip briefings for students and teachers, and final safety checks completed.

5

Educational Experience

Expert-guided tour with curriculum-aligned activities, hands-on learning, and continuous safety monitoring.

6

Post-Trip Evaluation

Learning assessment, certificates of completion, feedback collection, and planning for future trips.
