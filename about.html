Our Story & Leadership

Learn about Great African Adventure - Kenya's leading domestic and academic tourism company, led by visionary leaders with a passion for Kenyan exploration and education.

Leading Kenya's Tourism Revolution

Founded in 2014, Great African Adventure has established itself as Kenya's premier tourism company specializing in domestic and academic tourism. Our journey began with a simple yet powerful vision: to make Kenya's incredible natural and cultural heritage accessible to all Kenyans.

Today, we stand as a trusted partner for schools, universities, families, and organizations seeking meaningful travel experiences within Kenya. Our commitment to affordability, safety, and educational value has made us the preferred choice for institutions across the country.

Our Guiding Principles

The vision and mission that drive every decision we make

Our Vision

To be Kenya's leading domestic and academic tourism company, inspiring Kenyans to explore, learn, and appreciate their country's natural and cultural heritage while promoting sustainable tourism practices that benefit local communities.

Our Mission

To deliver affordable, well-organized, and educational travel experiences within Kenya that support learning institutions, conservation efforts, and local communities while maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and customer satisfaction.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our operations and define our company culture

Education Through Travel

We believe every journey should be a learning experience, combining adventure with educational value that complements formal learning.

Affordability & Accessibility

Making Kenyan tourism accessible to all through competitive pricing and flexible packages tailored to different budgets.

Safety & Professionalism

Maintaining the highest safety standards and professional conduct in all our operations, with comprehensive risk management protocols.

Cultural Appreciation

Promoting understanding and respect for Kenya's diverse cultures through authentic cultural exchanges and community engagement.

Environmental Responsibility

Practicing and promoting sustainable tourism that protects Kenya's natural beauty and supports conservation efforts.

Community Support

Investing in local communities through employment, partnerships, and support for community development projects.

Our Journey

Milestones in our growth and development as Kenya's tourism leader

2014

Company Foundation

Great African Adventure was founded by Mosese Ouma with a vision to make Kenyan tourism accessible to local communities and educational institutions.

2016

Academic Tourism Focus

Developed specialized curriculum-aligned programs for schools and universities, becoming a preferred partner for educational institutions.

2018

National Expansion
