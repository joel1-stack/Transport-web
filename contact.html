Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about booking and planning your tour

How far in advance should I book a tour?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for academic tours and 1-2 months for domestic tours. For peak seasons (July-August, December-January), we suggest booking 3-4 months in advance to secure preferred dates and accommodations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, M-Pesa, credit/debit cards, and cash payments. For schools and institutions, we can arrange invoice-based payments with flexible payment plans. A deposit is required to confirm bookings, with the balance due before departure.

Do you provide customized itineraries for schools?

Yes! We specialize in creating curriculum-aligned itineraries for schools. Our team works with teachers to design educational tours that meet specific learning objectives. We provide pre-trip educational materials, risk assessments, and all necessary documentation for school approvals.

What safety measures do you have in place?

Safety is our top priority. We are KTB licensed, fully insured, and all our guides are first aid certified. Our vehicles undergo regular safety inspections and are equipped with GPS tracking, first aid kits, and emergency communication systems. We maintain a 1:10 guide-to-student ratio for school tours.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We cater to all dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, and any allergies. Please inform us of any dietary restrictions during booking so we can make appropriate arrangements with our accommodation and restaurant partners.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer flexible cancellation policies. Full refund for cancellations made 30+ days before departure (minus any non-refundable expenses). 50% refund for 15-29 days notice. No refund for cancellations within 14 days of departure. For school groups, we work with institutions to reschedule where possible.
