Best Time to Visit

Plan your trip according to Kenya's seasons and wildlife migration patterns

Dry Season (June - October)

The peak tourism season in Kenya, featuring excellent wildlife viewing conditions, minimal rainfall, and comfortable temperatures. This is the best time for safari adventures and beach holidays.

Highlights: Great Wildebeest Migration in Maasai Mara (July-October), optimal game viewing, clear skies for photography, and pleasant beach weather.

Maasai Mara

Perfect for witnessing the Great Migration and predator action

Amboseli

Clear views of Mount Kilimanjaro and excellent elephant sightings

Diani Beach

Ideal beach weather with warm temperatures and minimal rainfall

Short Rains Season (November - December)

A pleasant time to visit with occasional short rains, lush green landscapes, and fewer tourists. The rains are typically brief and occur in the afternoon.

Highlights: Beautiful green scenery, bird watching opportunities, lower prices, and comfortable temperatures for exploration.

Lake Nakuru

Excellent for flamingo viewing and bird watching

Tsavo National Parks

Lush vegetation and good wildlife sightings

Coastal Regions

Warm temperatures with occasional refreshing showers

Wet Season (March - May)

The long rains season with heavier rainfall, particularly in April and May. This is the low season for tourism, offering significant discounts and unique experiences.

Highlights: Dramatic landscapes, newborn animals, excellent photographic opportunities with dramatic skies, and significant cost savings.

Aberdare National Park

Beautiful waterfalls and misty mountain scenery

Mount Kenya

Challenging but rewarding hiking conditions

Cultural Sites

Ideal for indoor cultural experiences and museum visits

Hot Season (January - February)

Warm and dry weather with hot days and pleasant evenings. This is an excellent time for beach holidays and wildlife viewing in most parks.

Highlights: Warm beach weather, good wildlife concentrations around water sources, and excellent conditions for water sports.

Malindi & Watamu

Perfect for snorkeling, diving, and beach relaxation

Samburu National Reserve

Specialized wildlife viewing in northern Kenya

Maasai Mara

Good general wildlife viewing before the migration
